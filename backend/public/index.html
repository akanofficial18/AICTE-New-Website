The Approval Journey

Every application moves through the same accountable stages. Each stage belongs to a defined role and is recorded in the audit trail.

Scrutiny

How the portal works

  1. Click Login to Portal and sign in with your face, a fingertip gesture or password + OTP.
  2. You land directly on your role-bound PS 26262 dashboard workspace with strict account and data isolation.
  3. Work your queue: scrutinise, review, route, approve or revert — every action is audit-logged.
  4. Track compliance, document validity, EVC visits and escalations from the sidebar.
